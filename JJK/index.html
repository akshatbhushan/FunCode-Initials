Jujutsu Kaisen

Jujutsu Kaisen is a story where sorcerers fight curses, born from negative human emotions, using a power called cursed energy. Among the strongest. JJK follows sorcerers who use cursed energy to fight curses born from human negativity. Key figures include Gojo Satoru, Sukuna, Yuji Itadori, Yuta Okkotsu, and Megumi Fushiguro.

Prominent Characters

Gojo Satoru

The strongest sorcerer of modern era.

The King of Curses, an ancient cursed spirit.
